# Game Production PM Method Cards

Use these cards only to interpret game-production and R&D project-management JDs and judge evidence quality. They are not candidate facts and must never be copied into resume claims.

## Card 1 — Version delivery evidence

- Decompose the role into version goal, scope and requirement breakdown, schedule and milestones, cross-functional dependencies, progress visibility, risk response, quality/delivery, and retrospective improvement.
- Strong evidence names the candidate's planning artifact or coordination mechanism and the resulting delivery state. Do not relabel event operations as a game version pipeline.
- Source: Ubisoft describes game project management as bridging creative, production, and business stakeholders, mitigating risks, and ensuring content quality: <https://www.ubisoft.com/en-us/company/careers/locations/articles/our-job-roles>.
- Recorded: 2026-08-22.

## Card 2 — Cross-discipline production

- Game production commonly connects design, art, engineering, product, and QA from concept through launch; assess whether facts prove cross-discipline delivery, not merely communication.
- Separate direct game-development evidence from transferable project delivery or live-event coordination.
- Source: Riot Games' Game Producer role describes cross-functional delivery with design, art, engineering, product, and QA: <https://www.riotgames.com/en/work-with-us/job/7961094/game-producer-event-wild-rift-contractor-shanghai-china>.
- Recorded: 2026-08-22.

## Card 3 — Agile evidence boundary

- Scrum evidence is stronger when facts show a visible ordered work list, a short-cycle goal, work decomposition, inspection of progress, adaptation, or a shared quality completion standard.
- General schedules, checklists, or weekly reviews are transferable evidence but must not be called Scrum or agile delivery unless the fact source confirms that method.
- Source: The official Scrum Guide defines transparency, inspection, adaptation, Sprint planning, visible backlogs, and Definition of Done: <https://scrumguides.org/scrum-guide.html>.
- Recorded: 2026-08-22.

## Card 4 — Capacity and game affinity

- Distinguish resource scheduling from team capacity, productivity, and talent-pipeline analysis. Do not infer the latter from staffing or shift planning.
- Treat played-game genres, game testing, localization, community analysis, and developer collaboration as different evidence types; none alone proves game-industry production experience.
- If formal game version production, capacity analysis, or industry internship is absent, state the material capability boundary once and keep the resume focused on the strongest transferable evidence.
- Recorded: 2026-08-22.
