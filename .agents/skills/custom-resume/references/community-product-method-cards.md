# Community Product Manager Routing Cards

Use these cards only to confirm `community_product_manager` and distinguish product evidence from operations evidence. They are not candidate facts or a finished writing strategy.

- Product evidence may include fact-backed user problems, requirement analysis, product proposals, interaction or rule design, delivery collaboration, iteration, and validation.
- Community operations, content publishing, event delivery, moderation, or feedback collection can support user understanding and operational collaboration.
- Operations evidence does not become product ownership unless confirmed facts also support the relevant product action and personal boundary.
- Keep `role_track` empty for this role family. Do not route it through `community_operations` merely because both roles work with communities.
