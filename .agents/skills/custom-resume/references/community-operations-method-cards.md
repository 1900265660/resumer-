# Community Operations Routing Cards

Use these cards to confirm the primary `community_operations` track before analysis. They define evidence boundaries, not candidate facts or finished writing strategy.

## Community

- Use `community` when the JD centers on member relationships, community mechanisms, moderation, activities, feedback loops, or community health.
- Content production and growth metrics may be adjacent evidence, but they do not replace community actions or ownership.

## Content

- Use `content` when the JD centers on topic planning, production, editing, distribution, content governance, or content performance.
- Publishing volume or reach does not by itself prove growth experimentation, conversion, retention, or recall ownership.

## Growth

- Use `growth` when the JD centers on acquisition channels, funnels, experiments, conversion, retention, activation, or recall.
- Community scale, content traffic, or activity delivery may be inputs; they are not direct growth ownership without fact-backed growth actions and result definitions.

## Integrated

- Use `integrated` only when the JD materially combines at least two of the above tracks and no single track dominates.
- `integrated` cannot turn unsupported adjacent evidence into direct ownership.

The coordinator proposes one primary track and obtains user confirmation. Other track requirements remain requirements or transferable evidence; they do not change the exemplar isolation key.
