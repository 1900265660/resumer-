# Community Role Content Judgment

Read this guide only for `community_operations` or `community_product_manager`. It controls evidence selection, writing ceilings, fusion, audit, and HR review; it is never a candidate fact source.

## Shared decision rule

Every positive role claim needs confirmed personal action, its object or method, and a result whose definition matches that action. Shared platforms, audiences, content, activities, or metrics can be adjacent evidence, but never create ownership for another track. When required action evidence is missing, keep the confirmed adjacent value within `writable_scope`, ask a fact question, or narrow the positioning.

## Community operations tracks

### `community`

Prefer member service or relationship work, community mechanisms or moderation, activities, feedback loops, and community-health decisions. Credible results include issue resolution, member satisfaction, participation quality, rule compliance, or an explicitly defined health metric. Member count, content volume, or one delivered activity does not by itself prove improved community health.

### `content`

Prefer topic/editorial planning, production or editing, distribution, content governance, and content-quality or consumption results. State the candidate's editorial and delivery boundary. Publishing volume, views, or reach does not by itself prove an acquisition experiment, conversion, retention, or recall result.

### `growth`

Require a defined funnel stage, hypothesis or experiment, concrete channel/action, metric definition, and supported result before using growth-owner language. Content traffic, community scale, or activity delivery can support a growth input but cannot become activation, conversion, retention, or recall ownership without those facts.

### `integrated`

Use only when confirmed facts contain direct actions from at least two operations tracks. Identify the primary and secondary evidence rather than flattening everything into “integrated operations.” Each result keeps its original definition. The label cannot fill a missing track or promote adjacent evidence.

## Community product manager

Direct product positioning needs confirmed user problem/research, requirement or solution definition, rule or interaction design, and delivery, iteration, or validation actions. A single experience may distribute these elements across several facts, but product ownership must still be attributable to the candidate.

Community service, moderation, publishing, activities, and feedback collection may support user understanding or operations collaboration. Without a confirmed product decision or artifact, write only the supported operations action and adjacent product relevance. Never infer requirement ownership, product proposal, interaction design, or version iteration from operations work alone.

## Resume structure and review

- Use `行业/平台经历` as the third self-ability heading and select it from the current frozen fact snapshot.
- Put the confirmed primary track in the first high-signal evidence; do not disguise it with a broader title.
- Keep result language tied to its measured object. Reach is reach, satisfaction is satisfaction, and conversion is conversion.
- Fusion must drop cross-track upgrades even when both drafts make the same unsupported inference.
- Auditor must fail truth for invented action, result definition, causality, or ownership; quality scores cannot offset that failure.
- HR Reviewer may return `strong_push` only when the primary role line, concrete action, credible result, and personal boundary are clear within a fast scan.
