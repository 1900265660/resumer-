# Game Designer Routing Cards

Use these cards to confirm the primary `game_designer` track before analysis. They define direction isolation, not candidate facts or finished writing strategy.

## Tracks

- `system`: rules, loops, resources, configuration, interfaces with numerical systems, and validation.
- `combat`: controls, skills, AI, combat encounters, parameter tuning, and combat validation.
- `writing`: original world, character, quest, system, or interface text; style rules; editorial delivery.
- `narrative`: narrative structure, character arcs, quest chains, branches, performance collaboration, and player-facing narrative experience.
- `general`: confirmed multi-direction design evidence when no single direction dominates.

## Isolation

- Play history and reviews are player/domain evidence, not design ownership.
- Localization or ordinary writing is content/quality evidence, not automatically original game-writing ownership.
- MOD or quality testing is testing evidence, not automatically combat or system design.
- Linear writing is not automatically branching narrative, quest-chain, or performance-system design.
- `general` cannot bypass a missing primary-direction action, method, artifact, or validation boundary.

The coordinator proposes one primary track and obtains user confirmation. Adjacent design evidence may remain transferable, but exemplars match only the confirmed track.
