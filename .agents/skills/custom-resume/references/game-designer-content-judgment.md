# Game Designer Content Judgment

Read this guide only for `game_designer`. It controls direction-specific evidence selection, writing ceilings, fusion, audit, and HR review. It is never a candidate fact source.

## Shared decision rule

A direct game-design claim requires a confirmed personal design action or artifact plus an appropriate validation or implementation boundary. Player history, reviews, content work, localization, and QA can establish industry understanding or transferable analysis/quality capability, but never create design ownership. Use the confirmed `role_track` as the exemplar and primary-scoring key; adjacent tracks remain adjacent.

## `system`

Prefer confirmed rule/loop, resource, configuration, state, progression, or interface-system design; require a specification, prototype, configuration artifact, or equivalent decision and a validation/iteration method. Player analysis and QA findings do not establish ownership of the underlying rules.

## `combat`

Prefer confirmed controls, skills, AI, encounter, behavior, or combat-parameter decisions plus playtest/debug/iteration. MOD testing, balance commentary, or submitted suggestions remain quality or adjacent combat evidence unless facts show the candidate made and owned the design change.

## `writing`

Prefer original game-facing text, writing briefs, world/character/quest/system/interface copy, style rules, editorial implementation, and delivery collaboration. Localization proves translation, editorial, consistency, and delivery actions; ordinary writing proves content craft. Neither automatically proves original game-writing ownership.

## `narrative`

Prefer narrative structure, character arcs, quest chains, branches/states, performance or implementation collaboration, and player-facing narrative validation. Linear prose, reviews, literary analysis, or ordinary content writing can be adjacent evidence but cannot become branch, quest-chain, or narrative-system design.

## `general`

Use only when facts show direct design actions from at least two tracks and no one track dominates. Name the primary and secondary evidence, retain each track's validation boundary, and do not use `general` to turn broad game familiarity, content, or QA into multi-track ownership.

## Resume structure and review

- Use `游戏经历` as the third self-ability heading, selected from the current frozen fact snapshot.
- Put the confirmed main direction and strongest owned artifact before general game affinity.
- State collaboration and implementation boundaries; do not attribute a team result solely to the candidate.
- Fusion must drop unsupported direction upgrades even when both drafts make the same inference.
- Auditor must treat invented action, artifact, validation, result, causality, or ownership as a truth failure.
- HR Reviewer may return `strong_push` only when the main-direction work, personal design decision, validation method, and interview-follow-up boundary are quickly identifiable.
