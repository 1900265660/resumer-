# Game-production Resume Content Judgment

Read this reference whenever `role_family` is `game_production_pm`. It refines content decisions; it does not change fact, approval, selection, or submission boundaries.

## 1. Fresh-fact invariant

- Re-read the current frozen fact snapshot before every JD analysis and every content revision. A prior resume, prior run, chat summary, or previously selected game list is never a candidate fact source.
- When the fact snapshot digest changes, rerun evidence mapping and selection. Do not patch the old wording in place.
- Prefer the newest confirmed exact value over an older approximation. Do not mix old rounded hours with newer precise hours.

## 2. Whole-resume hiring story

Build one game-production PM argument rather than a collection of game-adjacent facts:

1. WORK evidence establishes planning/delivery, coordination, process/quality, and risk handling.
2. Direct game PROJECT evidence establishes real game workflow, testing, localization, technical delivery, iteration, or player-facing outcomes.
3. `游戏经历` establishes target-product interest and product-category breadth; it cannot substitute for production actions.
4. `语言能力` states reusable, fact-supported work scenarios instead of copying project metrics.

Choose WORK/PROJECT entries by target-role actions and portfolio complementarity, not by attractive metrics or industry labels alone. When two game-adjacent projects overlap, keep the one that proves the more relevant action chain; do not preserve both merely to look more game-focused.

## 3. `游戏经历` decision policy

When the JD values rich game-product experience and the fact snapshot contains specific product evidence, a generic label such as “多品类深度玩家” is insufficient.

Rank candidate game facts in this order:

1. confirmed target-company or target-title experience;
2. accurately described adjacent products, genres, audience structures, or long-term product forms;
3. depth signals such as years, exact hours, level, story completion, collection, rank, or achievements;
4. representatives that add a genuinely new category or hiring signal;
5. differentiation and current relevance.

Do not rank by hours alone. A lower-hour narrative, live-service, multiplayer, or other category representative may add more decision value than another high-hour title from an already covered category.

Prefer one or two bullets when facts support both target depth and breadth:

- bullet 1: target product first, then accurately labelled adjacent or long-term products;
- bullet 2: confirmed total breadth where available, followed by a small set of category representatives with the strongest verifiable depth signals.

This is a hierarchy, not a fixed template. Use one bullet when evidence is genuinely short; use two when one dense list would hide the target product or make the breadth unreadable.

Avoid:

- calling products “同品类” when their core genres materially differ;
- listing every owned or played game;
- repeating a title whose stronger developer/project evidence already occupies scarce resume space unless the play fact adds a distinct hiring signal;
- retaining a common high-hour title when it adds less differentiation than another confirmed category representative;
- inferring system design, commercialization, player research, version operations, or product-analysis ability from play history.

## 4. Other self-ability entries

- `专业硬技能` and `综合软技能` must group reusable capabilities by work scenario; do not become unqualified keyword inventories or repeat entire project bullets.
- `语言能力` should lead with the confirmed credential, then state supported game-work actions such as reviewing English text/version material, identifying missing or changed text, and confirming issues with external parties. Do not duplicate counts already used as project scale unless the number changes the language judgment.
- Keep exactly `专业硬技能、综合软技能、游戏经历、语言能力`. Do not merge game and language.

## 5. Fusion and review checklist

Before passing a game-production draft, verify:

- current fact snapshot, not a prior run, determined the selected facts and values;
- the first high-signal content forms a target-role story rather than mutually distracting identities;
- direct game evidence and transferable general-project evidence are clearly distinguished;
- target product is visible before breadth when confirmed;
- product taxonomy is precise and the representative list is selective;
- repeated facts across PROJECT and self-ability serve different decisions;
- every play-history claim remains play history, not invented product competence;
- the result is interview-defensible and still scannable within the content budget.
